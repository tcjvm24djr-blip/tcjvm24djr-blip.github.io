Steam Hand Build Checklist

A cool, battery-safe way to get “steam” venting from a 3D-printed hand: an ultrasonic mist maker instead of real heat, so there’s no burn risk and no need for heat-shielding materials.

Materials to use

Interior materials

Interior materials
PartMaterialWhy
Reservoir / internal shellPETG filamentWatertight-friendly, tougher and more heat-tolerant than PLA
Reservoir sealSilicone sealant or O-ring gasketKeeps water from leaking into electronics
Vent channelsPETG or ABSHolds shape, smooth finish helps fog flow
Mist sourceUltrasonic mist maker disc (piezo transducer)Makes cool fog, no heat involved
AirflowSmall 5–12V DC fanPushes fog out through the vents
Power3.7V–12V battery pack (rechargeable Li-ion recommended)Powers disc and fan safely at low voltage
Wiring/insulationHeat-shrink tubing, silicone-sleeved wireBasic protection even though no real heat is involved

Exterior materials
PartMaterialWhy
Outer shell / armour platesPLA or PLA+Easiest to print cleanly, good detail for Iron Man plating
High-wear outer jointsPETG or ABSMore impact- and flex-resistant where fingers articulate
Surface finishFiller primer + acrylic spray paintHides print lines, gives a smooth metallic look
Metallic lookMetallic or chrome-effect spray paint, or vinyl wrapClassic Iron Man finish
Vent trimHeat-resistant silicone or rubber trimLets fog escape without cracking paint over time
FastenersM2/M3 screws and heat-set brass insertsLets you open the hand for battery changes and repairs
